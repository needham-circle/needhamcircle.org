## Welcome to Needham Circle

We're so glad you're here!

Needham Circle is a new grassroots effort started by two Needham neighbors, Dina Creiger and Avery Newton, to help bring people together, strengthen local connections, and make it easier for all of us to feel a little more connected to the town we call home.

We believe community doesn't have to be complicated. Sometimes it starts with a simple invitation, a familiar face, a shared cup of coffee, a local event, or a reason to gather on the Town Common.

That's what Needham Circle is all about.

## What We're Building

Needham is already full of wonderful people, businesses, organizations, events, and ideas. Our hope is to help connect the dots.

We're creating a place where residents can more easily find out what's happening, share events, discover local resources, support local businesses, and get involved in ways that feel meaningful. Some ideas may be big community gatherings. Others may be small, simple moments of connection. We believe both matter.
